Robomasters

The Robomasters competition is a nondestructive combat robotics competition. The North America competion format has two teams with 3 mobile robots fight each other to hold a zone. The robots fight by firing 17mm projectiles at armor plates on enemy robots. Each succesful hit results in a reduction of health for the enemy robot.

My role on the team is a computer vision programmer. My responsibilities include developing computer vision algorithms for panel detection and tracking. I have also explored particle filtering for predicting enemy robot positions.

Armor Panel Position Tracking

  • The goal of this project is to use classical computer vision to identify enemy robot armor panels
  • The process involves finding bright lights, pairing the lights together, and then checking if the light pair is valid by comparing the center symbol with known logos
  • Once the panel is found, the distance from the camera in 3D space is found using the estimated four corners of the panel, and Perspective-n-Point pose estimation

Auto Aim

  • A continuation of the Armour Panel Position project
  • The goal of this project is to aim a turret autonomously, and shoot at an armour panel
  • The positional data of the panel is passed into a particle filter to predict the panel's velocity and the center of the robot
  • The timing of the shot and turret position is found with inverse kinematics
  • The turret is told to shoot when the predicted panel position is in front of the predicted robot center

Localization From Visual Markers

  • Created to localize an autonomous robot
  • Finds a visual marker using computer vision
  • Calculates a position in 2D space using the dimensions of the marker
